In the Beginning

Rent A Pet began with a family that loved dogs. George Smith and his wife Carol owned two Irish Setters who were beloved family pets.

While walking his dogs in the park, George noticed how much attention they attracted. A comment from a young woman who wished she could own a large dog sparked the idea for a new type of business.

George and Carol realized that many people might want the companionship of a pet without the responsibility of ownership.

Where We Are Today

After considering the advantages and disadvantages, the Smiths officially started Rent A Pet in the fall of 1980.

The business began by offering dogs for rent, giving customers companionship without the expense or long-term responsibility of pet ownership.

Over time, Rent A Pet expanded its services to include cats, horses, pigs, and snakes.

What Makes Us Unique

Rent A Pet has grown beyond its original location in Provo and now operates franchises in Logan, Bountiful, Salt Lake City, and St. George.

The company employs trained veterinarians and animal trainers to ensure the health and safety of every animal.

Customers include senior citizens, young adults, party hosts, and even film producers looking for trained animals.
