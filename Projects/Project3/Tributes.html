Tributes – Rent A Pet

Bessie Anderson

Living in a retirement community that does not allow pets, Bessie thought she could never enjoy the companionship of a dog.

Through Rent A Pet, she was able to spend time walking a lovable bassett hound named Sophie in the park.

She now enjoys weekly walks without the responsibility of feeding or caring for a pet.

Jason Hutchingson

As a college student living with roommates, Jason wanted the experience of having an active dog without permanent ownership.

Rent A Pet matched him with Kamaikaze, a German Shepherd who enjoys running, skiing, and playing Frisbee.

Jason even met his fiancée during one of their outings, making the experience life-changing.

Michael Pinette

Michael travels frequently for work and enjoys surprising friends with unusual pets.

Rent A Pet allows him to enjoy exotic animals like snakes and iguanas without worrying about their long-term care.

The service fits perfectly with his lifestyle and love for variety.

Melinda Morrison

Melinda grew up loving horses but never had the opportunity to own one.

Rent A Pet made her dream possible by providing horses delivered directly to the trails where she rides.

She is able to build her skills and enjoy horseback riding without the cost of ownership.
